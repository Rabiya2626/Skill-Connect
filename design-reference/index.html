How it works

From "I know this" to a booked session in three steps.

01 / List

Add your skills and interests

Build a profile with what you can teach and what you're hoping to learn — languages, code, music, exam prep, anything.

02 / Match

Find a peer or propose a trade

Search tutors by skill and availability, or offer a straight exchange: your Python for their guitar lessons.

03 / Meet

Book, learn, and rate the session

Pick a time slot, get a confirmation, and leave a rating afterward so the community keeps getting better matches.
